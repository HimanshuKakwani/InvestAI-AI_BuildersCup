# TRENT LIMITED — UNAUDITED CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR Q1 FY2026-27
**Filing Reference:** BSE Scrip Code 500251 / NSE Symbol TRENT (SEBI LODR Regulation 33 Filing)

- **Gross Revenue from Operations:** ₹4,104 Crore (Up +24.58% QoQ; Up +56.17% YoY vs ₹2,628 Crore in Q1 FY26).
