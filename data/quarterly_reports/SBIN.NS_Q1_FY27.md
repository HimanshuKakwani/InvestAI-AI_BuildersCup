# STATE BANK OF INDIA — REVIEWED STANDALONE & CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR Q1 FY2026-27 (QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2026)
**Filing Reference:** BSE Scrip Code 500112 / NSE Symbol SBIN (SEBI LODR Regulation 33 Filing)

## 1. Quarterly Financial Performance Summary
- **Total Interest & Operating Income (Revenue):** ₹97,814.29 Crore (Up +10.24% YoY vs ₹88,725.36 Crore in Q1 FY26; -3.27% QoQ vs ₹1,01,125.59 Crore in Q4 FY26 due to seasonal Q4 fee spikes).
- **Net Interest Income (NII):** ₹41,125 Crore | Domestic Net Interest Margin (NIM): 3.35%.
- **Profit After Tax (Net Profit / PAT):** ₹24,113.00 Crore — Highest-ever quarterly Net Profit in SBI's history (Up +22.76% QoQ vs ₹19,642.87 Crore in Q4 FY26; Up +13.73% YoY vs ₹21,201.47 Crore in Q1 FY26).
- **Quarterly Earnings Per Share (EPS):** ₹27.02 (Trailing 12M EPS: ₹93.30).
- **Asset Quality & Capital Adequacy:**
  - Gross NPA (GNPA): 2.21% (improved by 55 bps YoY).
  - Net NPA (NNPA): 0.57% (improved by 14 bps YoY).
  - Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR incl. AUCA): 91.80% | Credit Cost: 0.38%.
  - Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR / CRAR): 14.28% | Return on Equity (ROE): 19.42%.
  - Promoter (Government of India) Shareholding: 57.23% (0.0% pledged).

## 2. Segment-Wise Quarterly Performance
1. **Retail Personal Banking:** Revenue ₹44,210 Crore | Operating Profit ₹11,850 Crore. Home loan growth +14.2% YoY with 0.68% GNPA.
2. **Corporate & Wholesale Banking:** Revenue ₹36,840 Crore | Operating Profit ₹10,920 Crore. Infrastructure and manufacturing credit up +16.1% YoY.
3. **Treasury Operations:** Revenue ₹14,120 Crore | Operating Profit ₹4,380 Crore. Aided by sovereign bond yield compression.
4. **SME & Agricultural Banking:** Revenue ₹2,644.29 Crore | Operating Profit ₹963 Crore. Digital SME loans via YONO Business grew +18.4% YoY.
